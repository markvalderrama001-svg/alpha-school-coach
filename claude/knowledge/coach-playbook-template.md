## 2. Guide philosophy

- **I don't teach content.** The practice app teaches. The tutor project helps when they're stuck. I set goals, check in, pay rewards and protect focus.
- **High standards + high support.** Never one without the other.
- **Reward the work they control** (mastered lessons, finished sessions), not test scores.
- **Short and consistent beats long and occasional.**
- **Keep it positive.** This is a family routine, not a second school.

## 3. Mentor language

| Situation | Try saying |
|---|---|
| Stuck | "This is a hard one. Show me the last step that made sense." |
| Got it wrong | "Talk me through what you did here." |
| Wants to quit early | "You're one lesson away. Finish it and you're free." |
| Pushed through | "You kept going when it got hard. That's the skill." |
| Rushing | "Fast doesn't earn anything. Correct does. Read it again." |
| Frustrated | "Five-minute break. Then one more and I'll sit right here." |

Praise effort and strategy, never "you're so smart."

## 5. Weeknight routine (30 to 35 min)

1. **2 min check-in:** "What's your goal tonight?" Start a visible timer.
2. **25 min focus block:** practice app only. Phones in another room, same room as a parent, screen visible.
3. **Stuck for 2+ minutes:** try it on paper first, then the tutor project. Hints, never answers.
4. **2 min wrap-up:** "What did you finish? What clicked?" Log it.

## 6. Weekend routine (60 to 90 min)

1. 15 min weekly goal meeting per learner
2. 25 min focus block
3. 5 to 10 min break, outside if possible
4. 25 min second block
5. Wrap-up, log, payout

## 8. Stall and gaming signals

| Signal | Likely cause | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Under 70% for 2+ sessions | Real gap or too hard | Sit with them for one lesson; go back a skill; shrink the goal |
| Missed goal 2 weeks running | Goal too big, fatigue, reward not worth it | Cut goal by a third; ask what would make it worth it; check sleep and homework load |
| Fast answers, low accuracy | Rushing for rewards | Name it calmly; speed isn't rewarded |
| Avoiding or arguing | Burnout or bad timing | Change the time; 15-minute sessions for a week |
| Asking the tutor for answers | Shortcutting | Written attempt first; read the tutor log together |
| Everything at 95%+ | Too easy | Let them move ahead |

Never punish with more work. Reset smaller and rebuild momentum.
